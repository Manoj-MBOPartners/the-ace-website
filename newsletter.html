NEWSLETTER

Welcome to the FIRST  EDITION newsletter of The ACE Apartment

This newsletter is a new step towards reflecting on the work already carried out and the progress made within our community. 

It brings together completed initiatives, collective efforts, and shared learnings that continue to shape everyday life at The ACE Apartment.

About This Newsletter

This newsletter serves as a reflective snapshot of our community’s progress and the work already accomplished.

It highlights how TAAOWA has been shaping systems, processes, and governance frameworks that support stability, accountability, and long-term sustainability.

Much of this effort happens quietly through coordination, planning, and resident cooperation; this publication brings that context together, acknowledges volunteers and staff, and reflects the shared direction in which our community continues to move.

TAAOWA TAKES CHARGE

REGISTERED & RECOGNISED

The Ace Apartment Owners Welfare Association (TAAOWA) was formally registered under the Tamil Nadu Societies Registration Act on 9 July 2025, marking the legal birth of a resident-run body to take charge of the community and its future.

SETTING THE FOUNDATION

Soon after registration, the interim association published the by-laws, created an official association seal, and launched a verified owners-only WhatsApp group to ensure transparent and direct communication with residents.

  • Interim association formally initiated the Handover–Takeover (HOTO) process with the builder.
  • Common area assets, services, and infrastructure were reviewed as part of transition readiness.
  • SNAG lists covering common areas, lifts, STP, WTP, seepage, and boom barriers were prepared.
  • Third-party infrastructure observations were conducted for STP, WTP, swimming pool, and lifts.
  • Builder was formally informed after association bank account opening to start fund and document handover.
    Builder had not transferred funds, maintenance records, or handover documents initially.
    • STP, WTP, and swimming pool systems were found built only to minimum statutory standards.
    • Seepage and leakage issues were observed in multiple common areas.
    • Limited admin control over existing MyGate, as it remained under builder ownership.
    • Lift-related gaps such as pending lighting, fans, and pit cleaning were identified. 


    • Common area snag lists were escalated to the builder’s Project Head and Legal team.
    • Seepage, leakage, and infrastructure gaps were formally notified for rectification.
    • Lift safety, panel replacement, and certification status were followed up with builder and vendors.
    • Builder was repeatedly reminded to initiate formal HOTO after bank account activation.
    • Vendor performance and KF contract expiry were flagged for transition planning.
    • Association bank account opened, enabling financial readiness for takeover.
    • All lifts certified safe for use, with key repairs completed and pending items tracked.
    • STP tanks cleaned, diffusers replaced, and operational parameters stabilized.

    HOUSEKEEPING TIGHTENS WASTE RULES

    TAAOWA reintroduced strict garbage segregation from October 15. Residents were asked to follow colour-coded disposal using green, blue and black bags to improve hygiene and streamline waste handling.

    COLOUR-CODED GARBAGE BAGS MADE MANDATORY

    To avoid mixing of waste, residents were instructed to use green bags for wet waste, blue for dry waste and black for sanitary or medical waste. Paper bags, loose cartons and unsegregated trash were clearly disallowed under the new system.

    Click to add image

    GARBAGE CHUTE TO OPEN ONLY AFTER SUCCESSFUL TRIAL

    Although chutes exist across towers, misuse before commissioning caused repeated mess. TAAOWA announced controlled trial runs handled only by housekeeping staff.

    COMMON AREA MISUSE DRAWS STRICT WARNING

    Housekeeping teams observed waste dumping in parking areas, OTS zones and corridors. Residents were formally cautioned against throwing garbage from balconies or leaving debris in common spaces, as it affects safety and neighbouring homes.

    SINGLE DUE DATE ANNOUNCED

    All maintenance and related dues were aligned to a single payment date of 1 March 2026. Residents were advised to verify invoices through MyGate or registered email communication channels.
