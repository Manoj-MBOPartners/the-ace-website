FAQ:

1. Why ₹0.5 per sq. ft.? The ₹0.5 per sq. ft. represents water charges that were billed separately earlier but are now considered along with maintenance based on GST clarification; please refer to the MyGate notice for a detailed explanation.

2. Why ₹4.5 per sq. ft.? The total maintenance is ₹4.5 per sq. ft., which includes ₹4.0 for common area maintenance and ₹0.5 for water charges; please refer to the MyGate notice for a detailed explanation.

3. Why two different invoices? Two invoices were issued — one for common area maintenance and one for water charges. However, after consulting GST experts, it was clarified that water charges are to be treated as part of maintenance costs. Accordingly, GST applicability is determined based on the combined amount if it exceeds ₹7,500 per month. Please refer to the MyGate notice for a detailed explanation.

4. Why this period? We all have handover at different points of time. This is the first invoice. Hence, this is raised for a singular period from the completion of your handover till Mar'2026.

5. Why only Rs. 4? This is only for maintenance. Water will be on actuals and shall be shared separately.

6. What types of payment are supported? We accept payments through MyGate, SBI Collect and Cheque (please check MyGate notice for details).
